1. Open index.html and en.html and scroll to the waiting list. Each
   language page embeds its matching Zoho form and provides a direct link.
2. Check the form fields, consent checkbox, and privacy links.
3. Submit one TEST entry for each form and confirm that it appears in
   Zoho Forms > Entries. Remove the test entries afterward.
4. Keep both forms publicly enabled in Zoho. If the embedded form does not
   load for a visitor, they can open the public form in a new tab.
5. The free photo-diagnosis button still opens an email draft to
   hallo@kastanie.wien. Customers must attach their own photos.
6. Before publishing, review the privacy policy and Zoho data-processing
   and international-transfer terms. Update legal details as needed.
7. Upload the entire website folder contents to the GitHub Pages repo.
   This ZIP does not deploy automatically.
