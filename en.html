For the curious

What makes houseplants struggle?

Not enough light

Our eyes compensate for brightness changes; plants cannot. A spot closer to the window can make a difference.

Flowers eventually fade

Many plants are grown for sale under near-ideal conditions. Your home may not provide the same light and warmth.

Dry heating air

Warm, dry winter air is tough on sensitive houseplants. Keeping them away from radiators can help.

Too much water

Slower growth often means less water is needed. Check the soil rather than watering only by schedule.
