TO PUBLISH
1. Back up your current website files.
2. Unzip this archive. Copy its CONTENTS to the root of your website repository (where index.html belongs).
3. Do not rename the asset directories or CNAME file.
4. Deploy through your existing website hosting process (for example, your current GitHub Pages workflow).
5. Refresh the live site (hard refresh if necessary).
6. Check index.html, en.html, the mobile layout, language links, photo contact dialog and both Zoho forms.
7. Check the privacy/legal information and licensing/attribution terms for all supplied stock photos.

IMPORTANT
This ZIP is a website package, not an automatic deployment.
The external Zoho forms and delivery of email enquiries need live-device/live-site testing.
Preserve the current form URLs unless you update your Zoho Forms configuration.
